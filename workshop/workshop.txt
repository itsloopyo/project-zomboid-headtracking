description=A graphics and gameplay overhaul for Project Zomboid that adds a first person mode and OpenTrack compatible head tracking with microphone lip syncing for maximum immersion. Every piece optional.

description=[h1]What it adds[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Optional head tracking with any OpenTrack compatible tracker: the view turns and leans with your head, your mouse or controller keeps the aim.
description=[*] 3D buildings, furniture, trees, hills and distant scenery, with new textures, shadows and reflections (Medium and High).
description=[*] Rain and snow that settle, with footprints and tyre tracks in the snow, lightning in storms, and trees that turn and drop their leaves in autumn (Medium and High).
description=[*] Modelled guns that kick and reload by hand (Medium and High).
description=[*] Shots hit the body part you aim at. In single player, limbs can be shot off and close shotgun blasts can burst a head.
description=[*] In single player, rounds go through plasterboard, wood, doors and glass, and break lamps and headlights.
description=[*] Bullet holes, and impacts that throw dust, splinters, sparks or blood (Medium and High).
description=[*] Fire simulation that lights its surroundings (Medium and High).
description=[*] Modelled vehicle interiors with working gauges, mirrors and wipers. Shoot from any seat.
description=[*] With head tracking, your character's head and flashlight turn with yours.
description=[*] Sound follows where you look, and is muffled by walls.
description=[*] Up to four player couch co-op, each with their own first person view, head tracking and lip syncing.
description=[*] Works online with no mod required on the server.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Controls[/h1]
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Key[/th][th]Action[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Delete[/td][td]Switch between first person and the isometric view. In split screen, see below. On a controller, double-tap Back[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Page Up[/td][td]Track head rotation and movement, rotation only, or movement only[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Page Down[/td][td]Switch between horizon-relative and camera-relative head turns[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Insert[/td][td]Cycle aim-down-sight modes[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F9[/td][td]Change graphics quality[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F8[/td][td]Change picture style[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F7[/td][td]Switch between vertical and horizontal split screen[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=In split screen, tap Delete, F9 or F8 once per player number to change that player, or hold it to change everyone.
description=
description=Insert goes through Sights locked, Free look with a marker and Stock sights. F9 goes through Automatic, Low, Medium, High and Custom. Keys can be changed in CameraUnlock.ini beside the game executable.
description=
description=In first person, put the reticle on a door, container or item and press your interact key (E by default). Click middle mouse for the pointer, then right-click anything for the game's own menu.
description=
description=[h1]Play it your way[/h1]
description=Head tracker, microphone, first person and new graphics are all optional.
description=[list]
description=[*] The game starts in its own isometric view, in Low by default. Set it to Medium or High in Options > Mods > Head Tracking for the mod's lighting, models and textures there too.
description=[*] Low keeps the game's own look in first person, apart from 3D furniture, windows and doors. Any of the mod's models, pictures and skins can be put back to the game's own on the same page.
description=[*] First person plays with mouse and keyboard or a controller, no tracker needed.
description=[*] Talking mouths is off until you tick it, and no microphone is opened until then. Sound is never recorded or sent: other players with this mod see only your head's turn and your mouth's shape.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Gameplay overhaul, offline[/h1]
description=Offline, shots go through thin walls, cripple and take off limbs, and vehicles knock down fences and lamp posts and drive through bushes. Each can be unticked in Options > Mods > Head Tracking. On a server they stay at the game defaults.
description=
description=[h1]Requirements[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] ZombieBuddy 2.x (tested with 2.3.4), set up once as its Workshop page describes. ZombieBuddy 3.0 is not supported yet.
description=[*] For head tracking only: a tracker, see Free head tracking below.
description=[*] Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Free head tracking[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Webcam: [url=https://github.com/opentrack/opentrack/releases]OpenTrack[/url] with Input neuralnet tracker and Output UDP over network, 127.0.0.1, port 4242.
description=[*] Phone: [url=https://headcam.app]Headcam[/url] (my own free app), pointed at your PC's LAN IP on port 4242. Allow the game through the Windows firewall when asked.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Installation[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Subscribe to ZombieBuddy and to this mod.
description=[*] Enable both in the game's Mods menu and restart the game.
description=[*] With ZombieBuddy's default settings, it asks you to approve this mod's Java code the first time and after each update.
description=[/list]
