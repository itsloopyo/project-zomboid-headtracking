version=1
title=Project Zomboid Head Tracking
description=An unofficial head tracking mod for Project Zomboid that moves the view with your head while your mouse or controller keeps aiming, driven by a webcam, phone, or any OpenTrack compatible tracker, with no VR headset required.
description=
description=It adds a first person view to the game, with a rebuilt world and new graphics.
description=
description=[h1]Head tracking and first person are both optional[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] First person: the game starts in its own isometric view every time. Press Delete to switch to first person, and again to switch back.
description=[*] Head tracking: first person plays with a mouse and keyboard or a controller, with or without a tracker. Without a tracker, nothing follows your head.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Requirements[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] ZombieBuddy 2.x (tested with 2.3.4), set up once as its Workshop page describes. ZombieBuddy 3.0 is not supported yet.
description=[*] For head tracking only: OpenTrack with a tracking source, or an app that sends the OpenTrack UDP protocol to port 4242 on this PC.
description=[*] Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Installation[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Subscribe to ZombieBuddy and to this mod.
description=[*] Enable both in the game's Mods menu and restart the game.
description=[*] With ZombieBuddy's default settings, it asks you to approve this mod's Java code the first time and after each update.
description=[/list]
