title=PZHT - First Person + Graphics & Gameplay Overhaul + Head Tracking
description=A graphics and gameplay overhaul for Project Zomboid that adds a first person mode with extensive retexturing and prop modelling, a bunch of gameplay tweaks, and OpenTrack compatible head tracking with microphone lip syncing. All features are optional. Anything that doesn't give an unfair advantage in PVP works online on unmodded servers.
description=
description=[b]This Workshop copy needs ZombieBuddy[/b], a separate mod by zed-0xff that loads Java mods into the game. It is not mine and I can't take responsibility for it: read its Workshop page before you install it. To play PZHT without ZombieBuddy, follow the installation instructions in the [url=https://github.com/itsloopyo/project-zomboid-headtracking]source repository[/url].
description=
description=It's free and open source (MIT) on [url=https://github.com/itsloopyo/project-zomboid-headtracking]GitHub[/url].

description=[h1]What it adds[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Optional head tracking with any OpenTrack compatible tracker: the view turns and leans with your head, while your mouse or controller aims.
description=[*] 3D buildings, furniture, trees, hills and distant scenery, with new textures, shadows and reflections (Medium and High).
description=[*] Enhanced rain and snow, with footprints and tyre tracks in the snow, lightning in storms, and trees that turn and drop their leaves in autumn (Medium and High).
description=[*] Modelled guns that kick and reload (Medium and High).
description=[*] Shots hit the body part you aim at. In single player, limbs can be shot off and close shotgun blasts can burst a head.
description=[*] Offline and on servers without PvP, rounds go through plasterboard, wood, doors and glass. In single player they also break lamps, headlights and window glass.
description=[*] Pools of lamp light, and mirrors and window glass that reflect you (Medium and High).
description=[*] Bullet holes and impacts that throw dust, splinters, sparks or blood (Medium and High).
description=[*] The game's original flames, with smoke, embers and firelight (Medium and High).
description=[*] Modelled vehicle interiors with working gauges, mirrors and wipers. Shoot from any seat.
description=[*] With head tracking, your character's head and flashlight turn with yours.
description=[*] Sound follows where you look, and is muffled by walls.
description=[*] Up to four player couch co-op, each with their own first person view, head tracking and lip syncing.
description=[*] Works online with no mod required on the server.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Controls[/h1]
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Key[/th][th]Action[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Delete[/td][td]Switch between first person and the isometric view. In split screen, see below. On a controller, double-tap Back[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F9[/td][td]Change graphics quality of the view you are in[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F8[/td][td]Change picture style[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F7[/td][td]Arrange two-player split screen side by side or above and below[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Page Up[/td][td]Track head rotation and movement, rotation only, or movement only[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Page Down[/td][td]Switch between horizon-relative and camera-relative head turns[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Insert[/td][td]Cycle aim-down-sight modes[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=In split screen, tap Delete, F9 or F8 once per player number to change that player, or hold it to change everyone.
description=
description=Insert goes through Sights locked, Free look with a marker and Stock sights. F9 goes through Automatic, Low, Medium, High and Custom for the view you are in, with no Automatic in the isometric view. Keys can be changed in CameraUnlock.ini beside the game executable.
description=
description=In first person, put the reticle on a door, window, container, item, radio, TV or crafting station and press your interact key (E by default). On an open window it climbs through. Click middle mouse for the pointer, then left-click to use or right-click for the game's own menu.
description=
description=[h1]Play it your way[/h1]
description=Head tracker, microphone, first person and new graphics are all optional.
description=[list]
description=[*] The game starts in its own isometric view, in Low graphics (vanilla) by default. Use F9 or set it to Medium or High in Options > Mods > Head Tracking to use the mod's lighting, models and textures in isometric view too.
description=[*] Low keeps the game's own look in first person, apart from 3D furniture, windows and doors. Any of the mod's models, pictures and skins can be put back to the game's own on the same page.
description=[*] First person plays with mouse and keyboard or a controller, no tracker needed.
description=[*] Talking mouths is off until you tick it, and no microphone is opened until then. Sound is never recorded or sent: other players with this mod see only your head's turn and your mouth's shape.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Gameplay overhaul, offline[/h1]
description=Offline, shots go through thin walls, break lamps and windows, and cripple and take off limbs, vehicles smash through fences, lamp posts, barricades and bushes, and in the isometric view zombies show as soon as your character could see them. Each can be unticked in Options > Mods > Head Tracking. On a server they stay at the game defaults, except that on servers without PvP, shots still go through thin walls and doors and past tree trunks, and zombies still show up straight away.
description=
description=[h1]Requirements[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] ZombieBuddy 2.x (tested with 2.3.4), set up once as its Workshop page describes. ZombieBuddy 3.0 is not supported yet.
description=[*] For head tracking only: a tracker, see Free head tracking below.
description=[*] For first person: OpenGL 3.3. Without it first person stays off and the screen says so.
description=[*] Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Free head tracking[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Webcam: [url=https://github.com/opentrack/opentrack/releases]OpenTrack[/url] with Input neuralnet tracker and Output UDP over network, 127.0.0.1, port 4242.
description=[*] Phone: [url=https://headcam.app]Headcam[/url] (my own free app), pointed at your PC's LAN IP on port 4242. Allow the game through the Windows firewall when asked.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Installation[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Subscribe to ZombieBuddy and to this mod.
description=[*] Enable both in the game's Mods menu and restart the game.
description=[*] With ZombieBuddy's default settings, it asks you to approve this mod's Java code the first time and after each update.
description=[/list]
