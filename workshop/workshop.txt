title=Project Zomboid Head Tracking
description=A graphics overhaul for Project Zomboid that adds a first person mode and OpenTrack compatible head tracking with microphone lip syncing for maximum immersion. Every part is optional.
description=
description=Free and open source under the MIT licence, the source is on [url=https://github.com/itsloopyo/project-zomboid-headtracking]GitHub[/url].
description=
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/itsloopyo/project-zomboid-headtracking/main/workshop/gallery/snow-sunset.jpg[/img]
description=
description=[h1]What it adds[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] 3D buildings, furniture, trees, hills and distant scenery, with new textures, shadows and reflections (Medium and High).
description=[*] Modelled guns that kick and reload by hand (Medium and High).
description=[*] Shots hit the body part you aim at. In single player, limbs can be shot off and close shotgun blasts can burst a head.
description=[*] Fire simulation that lights its surroundings (Medium and High).
description=[*] Fully modelled vehicle interiors with functional instrumentation.
description=[*] Sound that follows where you look, muffled by walls.
description=[*] Up to four player couch co-op, each with their own first person view, head tracking and lip syncing.
description=[*] Works online with no mod required on the server.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Controls[/h1]
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Key[/th][th]Action[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Delete[/td][td]Switch between first person and the isometric view (double-tap Back on a controller)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Page Up[/td][td]Track head rotation and movement, rotation only, or movement only[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Page Down[/td][td]Switch between horizon-relative and camera-relative head turns[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Insert[/td][td]Cycle aim-down-sight modes[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F9[/td][td]Change graphics quality[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F8[/td][td]Change picture style[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F7[/td][td]Switch between vertical and horizontal split screen[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=In first person the mouse turns the view and movement keys follow the direction you face. Put the reticle on a door, container or item and press your interact key (E by default) to use it. Click middle mouse to bring out the pointer, then right-click anything in view for the game's own menu.
description=
description=Keys can be changed in CameraUnlock.ini beside the game executable.
description=
description=[h1]Play it your way[/h1]
description=You don't need a head tracker or a microphone, you don't need to use first person, and you don't need to enable enhanced graphics. Every component is optional.
description=[list]
description=[*] Isometric with new graphics: the game starts in its own isometric view every time, in low quality by dfefault. Set the isometric view to Medium or High in Options > Mods > Head Tracking to use the mod's lighting, shadows, models and textures there too.
description=[*] First person with the game's own look: press Delete to switch to first person, and again to switch back. Low keeps the game's own look, apart from furniture, windows and doors, which are 3D in every mode. Any of the mod's own models, pictures and skins can be put back to the game's own on the same options page.
description=[*] No head tracker needed: first person plays with a mouse and keyboard or a controller. Without a tracker, head tracking does nothing.
description=[*] No microphone needed: Talking mouths, which moves your character's mouth as you speak, is off until you tick it, and no microphone is opened until then. The sound is measured as it comes in and is never recorded or sent: online, only how open your mouth is and the shape of your lips go to other players.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Always on, offline[/h1]
description=Offline the mod also changes some of the game's rules, and these have no switch: shots go through thin walls and doors, break lamps, cripple zombies' limbs and can take them off; vehicles knock down fences and street lamp posts and drive through bushes. On a server these stay at the game defaults.
description=
description=[h1]Requirements[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] ZombieBuddy 2.x (tested with 2.3.4), set up once as its Workshop page describes. ZombieBuddy 3.0 is not supported yet.
description=[*] For head tracking only: a tracker, see Free head tracking below.
description=[*] Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Free head tracking[/h1]
description=Both of these are free:
description=[list]
description=[*] Webcam: install [url=https://github.com/opentrack/opentrack/releases]OpenTrack[/url]. Set Input to neuralnet tracker and pick your camera in its settings, set Output to UDP over network, host 127.0.0.1, port 4242, and press Start.
description=[*] iPhone or Android: install [url=https://headcam.app]Headcam[/url] and point it at this PC's IP address (run ipconfig to find it) on port 4242. If Windows asks whether to let the game through its firewall, allow it, or the phone's data is blocked. The GitHub page has the command for adding the rule by hand.
description=[/list]
description=Any other OpenTrack compatible tracker works too.
description=
description=[h1]Installation[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Subscribe to ZombieBuddy and to this mod.
description=[*] Enable both in the game's Mods menu and restart the game.
description=[*] With ZombieBuddy's default settings, it asks you to approve this mod's Java code the first time and after each update.
description=[/list]
