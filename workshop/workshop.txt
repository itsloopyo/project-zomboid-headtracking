title=PZHT - First Person + Graphics & Gameplay Overhaul + Head Tracking
description=A graphics and gameplay overhaul for Project Zomboid that adds a first person mode with extensive retexturing and prop modelling, a bunch of gameplay tweaks, and OpenTrack compatible head tracking with microphone lip syncing. All features are optional. Anything that doesn't give an unfair advantage in PvP works online on unmodded servers.
description=
description=[b]This Workshop copy needs ZombieBuddy[/b], a separate mod by zed-0xff that loads Java mods into the game. It is not mine and I can't take responsibility for it: read its Workshop page before you install it. To play PZHT without ZombieBuddy, follow the installation instructions on GitHub.

description=It's free and open source (MIT) on [url=https://github.com/itsloopyo/project-zomboid-headtracking]GitHub[/url].

description=[h1]What it adds[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Optional head tracking with any OpenTrack compatible tracker: the view turns and leans with your head, while your mouse or controller aims.
description=[*] 3D buildings, furniture, trees, hills and distant scenery, with new textures, shadows and reflections (Medium and High).
description=[*] Enhanced rain and snow with footprints and tyre tracks, lightning, and trees that turn and drop their leaves in autumn (Medium and High).
description=[*] Modelled guns that kick and reload (Medium and High).
description=[*] Shots hit the body part you aim at. In single player, limbs can be shot off and close shotgun blasts can burst a head.
description=[*] Offline and on servers without PvP, rounds go through plasterboard, wood, doors and glass. In single player they also break lamps, headlights and window glass.
description=[*] In single player, rounds leave see-through holes in doors, walls and fences, knock out pickets and shatter door glass, and shotgun blasts tear gaps in wood. The damage stays in your save.
description=[*] Pools of lamp light, and mirrors and window glass that reflect you (Medium and High).
description=[*] In Medium and High, roads show worn wheel tracks, and in first person the lights nearest you cast shadows at night, stained glass colours the sunlight and rain wets your hands and weapon. In first person in High, wet roads, puddles, polished floors and glass reflect the surroundings.
description=[*] Gran Turismo 7's tone mapping, by Polyphony Digital, in first person above Low.
description=[*] Impacts that throw dust, splinters, sparks or blood, and in first person knock leaves off autumn trees (Medium and High).
description=[*] With sights up in first person, the background beyond your aim softens a little, never through a scope (Medium and High).
description=[*] In first person, a red arc at the edge of the view points to whatever hits you, and a faint glow warns of a zombie close behind you.
description=[*] In first person, thrown weapons go overarm to the reticle, and a fishing rod casts to the water under it.
description=[*] In first person, tap the map key to hold the map in your hands (not on PvP servers), and raise your arm to read your watch (off until you tick it).
description=[*] The game's original flames, with smoke, embers and firelight (Medium and High).
description=[*] Modelled vehicle interiors with working gauges, mirrors and wipers. Shoot from any seat. Offline, vehicles ride on springs over the hills, and in first person what you knock down breaks apart. You can also use first person only in vehicles.
description=[*] With head tracking, your character's head and flashlight turn with yours.
description=[*] Sound follows where you look, and is muffled by walls.
description=[*] Up to four player couch co-op, each with their own first person view, head tracking and lip syncing.
description=[*] Works alongside Project Viewpoint, whose camera turns with your head.
description=[/list]

description=[h1]Controls[/h1]
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Key[/th][th]Action[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Delete[/td][td]Switch between first person and the isometric view (Scroll Lock with Project Viewpoint). On a controller, double-tap Back[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F9[/td][td]Change graphics quality of the view you are in (Home with Project Viewpoint)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F8[/td][td]Change picture style[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]F7[/td][td]Arrange two-player split screen side by side or above and below[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Page Up[/td][td]Track head rotation and movement, rotation only, or movement only[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Page Down[/td][td]Switch between horizon-relative and camera-relative head turns[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]Insert[/td][td]Cycle aim-down-sight modes[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=In split screen, tap Delete, F9 or F8 once per player number to change that player, or hold it to change everyone.
description=
description=The first time first person shows, the mod picks Low, Medium or High for your PC. Keys can be changed in Options > Mods > Head Tracking, under Keys.
description=
description=In first person, aim at a door, window, container, item or station and press interact (E); a line under the crosshair says what it will do. On an open window, tap to shut, hold to climb through. Middle mouse brings up the pointer.
description=
description=[h1]Play it your way[/h1]
description=Every feature has its own switch in Options > Mods > Head Tracking, and Quick settings at the top turn whole groups on or off.
description=[list]
description=[*] The isometric view starts in Low (vanilla); F9 brings the mod's lighting, models and textures there too.
description=[*] Low keeps the game's own look in first person, apart from 3D furniture, windows and doors. Any of the mod's models, pictures and skins can be switched back to the game's own.
description=[*] First person plays with mouse and keyboard or a controller, no tracker needed.
description=[*] Talking mouths is off, and no microphone is opened, until you tick it. Sound is never recorded or sent: other players with this mod see only your head's turn and mouth's shape.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Gameplay overhaul, offline[/h1]
description=Offline, vehicles also smash through fences, lamp posts, barricades and bushes, and in the isometric view zombies show as soon as your character could see them. Each can be unticked. On servers these keep the game defaults, except that without PvP shots still pass thin walls and doors, and zombies still show straight away.

description=[h1]Requirements[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Tested on Project Zomboid 42.22.2.
description=[*] ZombieBuddy 2.x (tested with 2.3.4), set up once as its Workshop page describes. ZombieBuddy 3.0 is not supported yet.
description=[*] For first person: OpenGL 3.3. Without it first person stays off and the screen says so.
description=[*] Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Free head tracking[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Webcam: [url=https://github.com/opentrack/opentrack/releases]OpenTrack[/url], neuralnet tracker, UDP output to 127.0.0.1 port 4242.
description=[*] Phone: [url=https://headcam.app]Headcam[/url] (my own free app), sent to your PC's LAN IP port 4242. Allow the game through the firewall when asked.
description=[/list]

description=[h1]Installation[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Subscribe to ZombieBuddy and to this mod.
description=[*] Enable both in the game's Mods menu and restart the game.
description=[*] With ZombieBuddy's default settings, it asks you to approve this mod's Java code the first time and after each update.
description=[/list]
