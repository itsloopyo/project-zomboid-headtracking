# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Added a first person view. `F8` switches between it and the game's own view: the mouse turns the camera, the movement keys follow it, and the world is drawn from the game's loaded tiles. It stays off in multiplayer and split screen.
- Gave the first person view a picture of its own: a sun and moon that follow the game's clock and season, shadows, clouds, light shafts, rain, wet ground and water that mirror what stands over them, and the game's own characters and vehicles.
- Gave the land in the first person view a shape: hills, valleys round water, level ground under buildings, kerbs along streets and quays over water. It is drawn only. Movement, paths and collisions use the game's flat map as before.
- Made grass stand up in blades near the eye, put trees and bushes in leaf with depth to them, and let grass, earth and sand run raggedly into each other where the game lays them in squares.
- Gave buildings in the first person view walls with thickness, eaves, roofs closed on every side and power lines strung from pole to pole, and gave roofs, walls, floors, furniture, tree trunks and fences the grain of what they are made of.
- Added soft shadows that sharpen where a thing meets the ground, light that bounces between nearby surfaces, cloud shadows, polished floors, and anti-aliasing over time to the first person view. Looking down shows your character's body.
- Added a view from the seat when you are in a vehicle: the dashboard, wheel, doors and roof with the road ahead, seen through glass that carries a little dust and mirrors the sky at a glancing angle.
- Getting into a vehicle switches to first person, and getting out switches back to the view you had before.
- Headlights that are on light the road, the verges, characters and the air in front of the vehicle in first person, and the instruments glow in the cabin when the engine runs at night. Whatever stands in the beam throws its shadow down the road and through the lit air.
- Rain falling through headlights shows as bright streaks, stands as drops on a vehicle's windows, and is cleared from the front screen by the wipers.
- Night in first person is darker: the eye no longer opens up until the ground looks as it does by day.
- Rooms in first person are shaded in their corners.
- People and zombies in first person are lit by the sun and sky like the world round them, with smooth skins, soft shadow edges and a rim of sky light, and are dark at night outside a lamp's reach.
- A vehicle's cabin has grained trim and woven seat cloth, and each kind of vehicle has a driver's place of its own, with its own wheel, instruments, colours and gear lever: a stitched wheel with a padded hub and switches, stalks behind it, a hooded speedometer and rev counter with a fuel gauge, whose needles follow the vehicle and which light up at night, and a mirror on the screen that shows the road behind. The driver sits in line with the wheel, behind each vehicle's own wheel: higher in a pickup, further forward in a van.
- A room at night is dim unless something lights it.
- People and zombies near the eye in first person are rounded where the game's models are faceted.
- A clear day in first person has clearer air, fuller colour and more contrast, warm in the sun and cool in the shade.
- Vehicles in first person are the size they are in the game's own view. They were drawn half as big again.
- The game's own isometric view is drawn exactly as before.
- Centred the game window in its monitor's work area when the game runs windowed, at startup and after a resolution change. Fullscreen and borderless are left as the game places them.
